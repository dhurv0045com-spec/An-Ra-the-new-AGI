# ARK-016 NOVELTY

## Classification

**`UNKNOWN_STATUS` / `NOT_DEMONSTRATED` for mechanism credit.**

Applied-update trust regions, gradient/update clipping, and LR scheduling are known intervention families. ARK-016 was designed as a causal discriminator, not as a novelty claim.

Because only one qualifying recovery fork existed and all arms were stable, the experiment produced no evidence that can distinguish update-magnitude mediation from LR-specific optimizer dynamics.

The cap primitive and telemetry are useful infrastructure, but no new mechanism is claimed from this run.
