# ARK-010 RED TEAM

Pattern under attack: after post-G90 instability, continued HIGH LR reacquires sustained G90 more effectively than immediate LOW LR.

- Prospective collapse sources: PASS.
- Same source checkpoint and matched future tail: PASS by V5 runner design.
- Recovery incidence: HIGH 8/9 vs LOW 2/9.
- Nesting caveat: 9 recovery conditions come from only 3 independent acquisitions.
- State dependence: LOW being good before collapse and poor after collapse is not a contradiction.
- Missing decisive arm: HIGH until recovery, then LOW. Therefore an adaptive schedule is still only a hypothesis.
- Collapse is not permanent forgetting: most HIGH arms recover.
- Task/scale: canonical Micro T2 only.

**Verdict:** strong enough to motivate a preregistered adaptive schedule experiment; not a general hysteresis law.
