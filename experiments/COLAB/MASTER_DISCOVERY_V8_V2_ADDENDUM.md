## Promotion logic

A mechanism cannot move forward merely because it improves a toy robustness metric.

### Gate A — ARK-017
Must provide event-sufficient causal credit. If unresolved, ARK-018 runs without an optional mechanism arm and is treated primarily as a transfer/measurement study.

### Gate B — ARK-018
Must produce a valid data/checkpoint receipt and either:
- a protection benefit during real-text continuation without >5% SEALED real-text NLL penalty; or
- a clear negative/low-interference result that blocks Guardian intervention claims.

### Gate C — ARK-019
Guardian promotion requires stability, plasticity, language-quality, intervention-duty-cycle and exact-resume criteria simultaneously.

No single metric can promote the controller.

## Non-negotiable scientific boundary

The goal is not to maximize runtime or manufacture a positive result. The goal is to end with enough causal and real-data evidence to decide what, if anything, belongs in the next An-Ra training algorithm and instrumentation stack. Negative results are program progress.
