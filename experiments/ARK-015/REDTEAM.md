# ARK-015 RED TEAM

## Strongest alternative explanations

1. **Near-freezing:** LOW `1e-5` traversed ~100x less parameter path than NARROW_HIGH. LOW may protect because it barely changes the model, not because LR has a special consolidation effect.
2. **Stress-specificity:** the continuation intentionally removes order diversity. The result demonstrates retention under this distribution-narrowing stress, not arbitrary non-arithmetic forgetting.
3. **Micro/synthetic scope:** the task is non-arithmetic but still a small controlled symbolic binding problem. It is not natural-language reasoning.
4. **Data-support confound for AUGMENTED_HIGH:** AUGMENTED_HIGH changes presentation diversity by design. Its success shows movement alone is insufficient, but does not identify which gradient/statistical property of augmentation protects the invariant.
5. **Eight pairs are clustered:** there are 8 matched pairs but only 3 independent acquisition parents. Pair count must not be represented as 8 independent models.
6. **Budgeted missing ninth triplet:** seed 2503/order 8803 was not run. The preregistered positive criterion still passed without it; it must not be silently imputed.

## What would falsify the current interaction hypothesis

- HIGH with LOW-matched applied update magnitude still erodes invariance while LOW does not: weakens pure movement mediation.
- A small invariant-supporting replay fraction fails to protect despite AUGMENTED_HIGH protection: suggests the amount/structure of diversity matters more than a simple replay rule.
- On real data or a larger proxy, narrowed specialization does not produce robustness loss, or the LOW/replay interventions cease to help.
- The effect disappears with a different optimizer or after matching integrated update path.
