# ARK-015 NOVELTY

## Classification

**`NEW_EMPIRICAL_DISCOVERY` (program-local), with known intervention families.**

The individual ingredients are not novel: learning-rate reduction, data augmentation, replay/diversity preservation, and catastrophic-forgetting phenomena are established families.

What is new inside Arkenstone is the matched causal observation that a robust non-arithmetic binding capability can remain perfect on the narrow canonical distribution while its order-invariant solution collapses under HIGH LR, with LOW LR and continued augmentation both preventing the loss under matched semantic training.

## Evidence

- 3 independent fresh acquisition seeds;
- 8 SEALED-qualified matched primary pairs;
- NARROW_HIGH 8/8 failures;
- NARROW_LOW 0/8;
- AUGMENTED_HIGH_REFERENCE 0/8;
- ordinary canonical exact remained 1.0 while invariant accuracy eroded.

## Boundary

Novelty is **not** claimed for LR decay, data augmentation, replay, catastrophic forgetting, or invariance learning in general. No claim of a new universal mechanism is justified until the update-magnitude/data-support interaction is causally separated and survives real-data/scale transfer.
