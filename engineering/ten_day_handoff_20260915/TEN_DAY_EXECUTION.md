## Standing decisions for blockers and opportunities

- Missing code/test consumer: implement it. Do not ask the owner to approve a routine repair or replace the assignment with another estimate.
- Real prerequisite failure: work on independent owned tasks while resolving it; record the specific failing command/contract.
- Conflicting agent edits: resolve ownership before editing that file. Use Luna/Sol only for bounded independent work with clear exclusions.
- Local resource limit: measure the exact memory/disk/dependency limit, preserve partial evidence and provide a reproducible CPU route. Do not assume Kaggle is required for nonlearning work.
- Test hangs: stop only the owned child after its deadline; preserve output and repair progress/counters. Do not rerun indefinitely.
- Weak experimental accuracy with correct execution: valid negative result, not justification to change sealed data or thresholds.
- Good experimental accuracy: report both seeds, every comparison, cost and retention. Do not infer general intelligence from a narrow result.
- Creativity: after baseline acceptance, record an evidence-repair controller/identifiability-driven inquiry proposal in a future-work file. No new controller, objective or allocation inside the fixed campaign without a new registered plan and owner authorization.
- Work completed early: finish release/recovery validation, then stop. Do not manufacture tasks to consume time or tokens.
