## Additional data and semantics obligations

Prepared inventory/program submission actions use `answer`, while live environments require `item`/`value`; the same environment/action schema must generate training and evaluation traces. `build_batch_for_trajectory` trains the final answer from the initial public goal alone, without the received inquiry history needed in inventory. Rule trajectory target `mechanism.answer` is the hidden target constant, while live success is the result of `verify_rule` on the sampled world. Treat this as a task/label contract migration; do not patch only serialization.

Time-aware evidence needs explicit unknown-time handling. The current implementation orders arbitrary textual scopes and formats numeric times as strings, which can misorder negative values and collapse fine time differences. Do not create false knowledge of temporal order. Existing same-time, duplicate and transition tests are useful but incomplete.

E2 generates its own live mechanism stream instead of consuming the frozen confirmation inventory. That stream has a label but no demonstrated equivalence-group exclusion against the prepared training bundle. A new seed or generator label is insufficient split evidence. Bind every evaluated mechanism to a registered evaluation inventory and enforce canonical disjointness before running it. Count every admitted failure in the denominator; do not replace difficult episodes after observing their outcomes.
