# RESEARCH KILL CRITERIA

**Phase 2 · 2026-09-13.** Every expensive research line gets a pre-committed stop condition. Once the condition fires, the line stops **without renegotiation**; reopening requires a new preregistration that names what changed.

| ID | Research line | Kill criterion (pre-committed) | What we stop doing | What survives |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| K01 | Output-competition mechanism (R1C line) | R1C primary causal pair fails: mean paired MASK_4096-vs-FULL AUC gap < 0.10 AND no sustained-G50 advantage in ≥2/4 seeds, in both structural AND functional endpoints | Treat output competition as the primary formation explanation; design any output-space intervention into the next Core | The data remains valid; tied-geometry/init dissection becomes the successor line; R1B's curve stands as description |
| K02 | Guardian / dynamic controller line | (a) V4 bundle fails byte audit or cannot be produced AND a controlled rerun shows Guardian arms do not beat matched STATIC_REPLAY at equal retention; OR (b) in any future Guardian campaign, task-ID-hidden controller features lose the retention advantage | Internalizing any controller; ARK-021 designed against a dynamic controller; further controller variants | Static sparse replay as the default external protection; dose calibration (U05); escalation-as-recovery remains documented |
| K03 | Class-space transfer line | CS-TRANSFER-001 formation gap < 0.10 at 8L/256w on BOTH the scaled-arithmetic and production-tokenizer tasks | Treating vocabulary/output-space as a 500M-blocking variable; further transfer probes | The micro-scale effect stays demonstrated science; tokenizer returns to default-baseline status |
| K04 | Natural-text representation line | A production-tokenizer natural-text mini-probe shows < 0.05 formation difference across class-space treatments (after CS-TRANSFER-001 partial transfer) | Any natural-text tokenizer change proposal | Micro-scale representation research continues |
| K05 | Scorer-certification line | Two consecutive preregistered policy families fail the extended C0 bias screen after fixture redesign | Assisted-scoring attempts; candidate-ranking as a primary readout | Candidate-free generation evaluation remains THE primary; scorer research only as diagnostics |
| K06 | Self-model / learned-diagnosis line | A readiness-v2-qualified checkpoint's qualified IBQ basis still fails to beat constant baselines prospectively (two independent bases) | Training any neural self-model; diagnosis claims beyond instrumentation | External verifier/experimenter instrumentation (which is the actual workhorse) |
| K07 | 500M campaign authorization | Corpus gate open > 2 more research phases, OR representation decision (U01/U03) unresolved at PRE500M review | Scheduling any 500M-scale preparation beyond reversible engineering | Development-scale campaigns (R1C-class) continue — they are the actual science |
| K08 | Curriculum/teacher/data-staging line | (already fired) ARK-003 + T1D nulls | Staging-based acceleration attempts at micro scale | Data-diversity research (content, not order) remains open |
| K09 | Discovery-controller line | (already fired at current scale) BRAMASTRA nulls ×2 | Controller-vs-random comparisons below subject-qualification | Revisit only on a qualified substrate with a task distribution where random is measurably bad |
| K10 | Arithmetic micro-campaign line (Cymek GPU series) | After R1C + CS-TRANSFER-001: if both the mechanism question and the transfer question are answered, further arithmetic-micro campaigns without a new mechanism hypothesis |Incremental arithmetic reruns | The campaign harness remains for whichever successor question the outcomes nominate |

**Shared rule:** a kill criterion that fires on a single confounded or single-seed result does not count; the criterion must fire on the preregistered decision rule of a properly controlled experiment (or an audit of primary evidence), and the firing must be recorded in the evidence ledger.
