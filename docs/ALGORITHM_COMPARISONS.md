# Formation comparisons

The measured bottleneck is V4's zero QUERY decisions and 0/4 revision success under an untrained action-symbol mapping. ELD-DEV-004's alias and three-hop failures remain separate graph questions. This phase tests formation of a costed action policy before retention or forward transfer.

AdamW remains the unchanged optimizer control. Semantic decay exclusions retain RMS/QK scale handling and one owner for tied embeddings. There is no evidence here to promote a new optimizer. Schedules, precision, sampler, objective, tokenizer and data are recipe-bound. Stage changes use a typed parent checkpoint/recipe/data transaction, fresh child identity and explicit optimizer reset. A resume cannot silently become a task migration.

The synthetic action dataset has known, missing, conflicting, explicit-null and unavailable-evidence worlds. Initial labels derive only from public belief and channel availability. Post-query answers are labeled after the public observation arrives. The oracle is explicitly privileged; model-origin policies never receive truth or channel payloads before QUERY. Initial and post-query prompt hashes are split globally, independently of candidate outcomes. New world IDs, ordered observations, query-blind and recency controls supplement exact duplicate checks. This is one narrow development generator with status cues; semantic out-of-distribution transfer is not established.

On the ten-world validation instrument, the fixed public-evidence policy is 10/10 with four requests (utility 9.6), always-query 10/10 with eight requests (9.2), no-query 6/10 (6.0). Oracle success is separately attributed and unachievable without privileged truth. Random, recency and query-blind raw traces are retained. No fixed success is counted as model cognition. Query cost is 0.1; model calls/positions are reported separately rather than made invisible in that utility.

The cloud screen compares three arms at the same architecture, initialization seed, examples, supervised tokens and complete-epoch update budgets. Report each state kind, full EOS/invalid actions, first QUERY rate, redundant requests, total positions, wall/GPU seconds and checkpoint lineage. Confidence is raw joint output likelihood; its Brier value is explicitly uncalibrated. No awareness claim is admissible. Calibrated error estimation needs a separate validation-fitted calibration study.

A screen candidate must reach at least 90% complete-episode accuracy, at least 80% in every state kind, zero invalid actions and at most 0.05 excess requests per episode above the fixed policy, in both screen seeds. An alternative signals improvement only with at least 0.05 higher mean per-episode utility than CE in both seeds and no state-kind accuracy regression exceeding five points. These prospective gates must not change after viewing model outcomes. If all arms fail formation, diagnose raw outputs and overfit controls before increasing compute. If CE wins, retain it.

Tokenization is not included as an unfairly mixed arm in this first action study. The UTF8 reference expands vocabulary 46→260 and changes positions/embedding competition; it requires a separately matched representation experiment. Cymek's nonmonotonic class-space evidence and later consolidation rule out claiming that a smaller or larger vocabulary universally fixes learning. No external text/license claim is made. Future subword adoption must pin legally usable tokenizer assets, training corpus/license, normalization and full artifact hashes and compare equal semantic examples as well as tokens and measured work.

Fresh action-model weights are intentional formation controls. They do not replace the accepted two-read checkpoint, nor demonstrate its retention after action training. Both run through the same backend/episode interfaces. A later multi-task or warm-start comparison must qualify retained route skill and newly acquired actions together before adopting new weights as the integrated learner.
