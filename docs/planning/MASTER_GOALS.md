# Master Goals

## P0-01: Recover Native Language Coherence

Prove tokenizer competence, truthful checkpoint loading, deterministic generation,
and coherent dialogue before activating or promoting adaptive subsystems.

`iterate500` exists to make the native An-Ra model trainable, inspectable, and
evidence-gated on practical Colab hardware.

## P0: Preserve the Experiment

- Keep An-Ra on its own checkpoint, tokenizer, architecture, mathematics, data,
  and identity lineage. Do not replace it with external pretrained weights.
- Preserve every compatible tensor and canonical token ID through explicit,
  versioned migrations.
- Keep MoD, RIM, DSTP, ESV, HAL, memory, cognition, agents, verifiers, DFC, and
  ThirdEye connected, traceable, and independently measurable.

## P0: Recover Coherent Behavior

- Prove checkpoint and tokenizer identity before tuning generation.
- Establish deterministic diagnostic behavior with finite activations, explicit
  stop reasons, cache parity, and zero session leakage.
- Treat coherence below the recovery threshold as undertraining when artifact
  identity is exact.
- Continue the native checkpoint through raw foundation, staged subsystem,
  technical/DFC, instruction, and verifier-replay phases.

## P0: Measure Every Claim

- Expose exact prompt assembly, token allocation, subsystem execution, quality
  state, persistence, and release evidence in the Developer Matrix.
- Evaluate diagnostic, native, and full-system modes on private tasks across
  three seeds.
- Require positive ablations for every native subsystem and bounded latency.
- Promote only signed, reproducible bundles with verified rollback.

## P1: Scale Carefully

- Build immutable 15/30 GB corpus campaigns with provenance, deduplication,
  source-hash splits, tokenizer identity, and unique-token accounting.
- Use multiple Colabs as isolated experiment workers with unique artifacts.
- Increase context from 1,024 to 1,536 and 2,048 only after short-context and
  retrieval gates pass.
- Extend vocabulary only when append-only fertility evidence justifies it.

## Definition of Success

Success is not low training loss, a responsive UI, or subsystem execution alone.
It is a coherent checkpoint that passes artifact proof, recovery, private
capability, native ablation, full-system integration, human review, signed
promotion, and rollback gates.
