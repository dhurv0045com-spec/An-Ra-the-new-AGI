# Experiment protocols

ELD-DEV-001 through 004 are frozen historical studies. See their source SHAs, committed receipts, negative findings and checkpoint custody in the [evidence register](EVIDENCE_AND_PROVENANCE.md). V3 retains the old training recipes and scorer routes for controlled comparison.

The V3 campaign screens graph composition across held-out canonical graph topologies and factorized render shifts. Compare a fixed two-read candidate, conventional contextual pointer, an appropriately sized dense control, shared repeated reads, and rendering interventions as specified in the frozen operator matrix. Score complete free generation with EOS. Report both answers correct for each query pair, factorized alias and punctuation results, learned depth and depth extrapolation, held-out topology, shortcut controls and measured work. Do not treat related views or multiple training seeds as independent graph samples.

Freeze method, seed, dataset manifest, source, optimizer, model dimensions and steps before running. Run equal-example and equal-update contrasts first. Use measured training time and evaluation cost for separate cost comparisons. A profile change creates a separate experiment, even when methods share names. Cloud validation must establish hardware health and exact same-device restart before spending a campaign budget.

The highest priority criterion is a prospective improvement on the independent topology and representation shifts at matched work. Direct graph traversal and canonicalized input are explicit engineered strategies. Their success has a different interpretation from an improvement in learned raw-input invariance.

The Kaggle phase uses a separate development replication split and a stronger capacity lane. Confirmation under independently held labels remains a later, separately authorized gate.
