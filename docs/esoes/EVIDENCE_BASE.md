# ESOES Evidence Base

Version: 0.3
Updated: 2026-08-29

This is the distilled evidence ESOES may use. Prior implementations are referenced by immutable branch/commit paths; they are not V5 dependencies.

## Evidence labels

- **PROVEN / STRONG EVIDENCE** — direct receipt, replicated controlled result, or mature external evidence.
- **LIKELY** — supported inference with an important untested link.
- **SUGGESTIVE** — useful hypothesis, not a design foundation.
- **INVALIDATED / CONTAMINATED** — known defect, leakage, or failed claim.
- **UNKNOWN** — no adequate evidence.

## Internal An-Ra evidence

### V4 substrate and architecture

Reference: `core-frozen-v4@f72f193` and `core-vnext@054619f`.

- **PROVEN / STRONG EVIDENCE:** the canonical executed V4 dense Core has 180,093,312 trainable parameters: 18 layers, width 896, 14 query heads, 2 KV heads, head dimension 64, SwiGLU FFN 2432, vocabulary 32,768, context 2,048, RoPE, RMSNorm, QK norm, tied embeddings, and hybrid local/full attention.
- **PROVEN / STRONG EVIDENCE:** V4 can produce language-like continuation, but the preserved behavioral batteries did not establish robust instruction following, supplied-fact use, contextual binding, or composition.
- **UNKNOWN:** whether V4 failed because of capacity, architecture shape, data composition, token insufficiency, optimization, or interactions among them. V4 is not a clean ablation of any one cause.

### PGE continuation and provenance

Reference: `core-vnext@054619f:output/pge_audit/CANONICAL_REPORT.md`, `token_provenance_ledger.json`, `lineage.json`, and `RECEIPT_HASHES.json`.

- **PROVEN / STRONG EVIDENCE:** the audited step-20,000 → step-22,517 continuation changed model parameters and Adam state; it was real optimization rather than a no-op.
- **PROVEN / STRONG EVIDENCE:** recorded held-out LM loss improved from 2.1884 to 1.9710 while the target copy/context/binding/composition battery remained near zero or chance.
- **PROVEN / STRONG EVIDENCE:** step 21,800 behaved better than the final checkpoint on preserved tests. Final checkpoint is not synonymous with best checkpoint.
- **PROVEN / STRONG EVIDENCE:** 329,908,224 continuation tokens are certified. A 2.7B lifetime-token claim is not supported by the surviving provenance contract.
- **LIKELY:** the V4-scale model was not trained with enough verified, high-quality tokens to infer a capacity ceiling. The exact lifetime token total remains **UNKNOWN**.

### Training and evaluator failures

References: `core-vnext@4ee180a` history and `core-exp@51124de:PURPOSE.md`.

- **PROVEN / STRONG EVIDENCE:** device transfer once separated optimizer parameter identity from live model parameters; a run can advance nominal steps without updating the intended weights unless identity is tested.
- **PROVEN / STRONG EVIDENCE:** checkpoint export and download workflows previously produced incomplete or ambiguous artifacts. Disposable workers require durable full-resume state, not merely a filename.
- **PROVEN / STRONG EVIDENCE:** a label off-by-one defect, wrong-gold counterfactual scoring, and stateless decoding each produced misleading measurements while loss or surface output looked plausible.
- **PROVEN / STRONG EVIDENCE:** source commit, tokenizer, data manifest, model hash, optimizer step, cumulative tokens, and evaluator hash must form one lineage receipt.

### SFT6: query-conditioned preference

Reference: `core-exp@51124de:output/qim3_sft6_replication.json`, generated by source commit `6e1f4f8`.

- **PROVEN / STRONG EVIDENCE:** grouped counterfactual query-swap training produced a mean fact-group query lift of 2.5052 nats versus 0.0192 for its parent; paired mean delta 2.486, bootstrap 95% interval [1.7479, 3.258], 35/40 groups positive.
- **PROVEN / STRONG EVIDENCE:** candidate rank-1 reached 63/119, while corresponding greedy accuracy was only 24/119 in that receipt. Representation/selection and realization are separable.
- **LIKELY:** holding facts fixed and changing the query supplies a high-value causal learning signal for V5.
- **UNKNOWN:** whether the mechanism transfers from controlled nonce formats into broad natural reasoning during foundation training.

### SFT7: margin result, stated precisely

Reference: `core-exp@51124de:output/qim3_sft7_margin_replication.json`, corrected evaluator v3.1.

- **PROVEN / STRONG EVIDENCE:** relative to SFT6, the margin child increased mean group lift by 0.1049 nats, bootstrap 95% interval [0.0420, 0.1697], with 26/40 positive groups.
- **PROVEN / STRONG EVIDENCE:** on the independent QIM-v4 analysis recorded in `PURPOSE.md`, the intended selection hypothesis was falsified: rank-1 did not improve (66→64/119); greedy improved by 5/119 on both fixtures.
- **INVALIDATED / CONTAMINATED:** the blanket statement “SFT7 did nothing” is too strong. The correct conclusion is that same-block candidate margin did not solve the selection bottleneck and may have tightened decode commitment.
- **LIKELY:** a V5 auxiliary objective must manipulate the query-conditioned causal contrast rather than merely widen a same-query candidate margin.

### Counterfactual normalization and realization

Reference: `core-exp@51124de:PURPOSE.md` and its cited QIM-v5/QIM-v6 artifacts.

- **PROVEN / STRONG EVIDENCE:** on QIM-v5, counterfactual normalization changed raw 70/149 to normalized 106/149 under clean reproduction.
- **PROVEN / STRONG EVIDENCE:** constrained decoding repaired 15 of 19 rank-1 realization failures in the cited regime, but did not solve general selection.
- **PROVEN / STRONG EVIDENCE:** early adaptive policies failed fresh transfer; distribution transfer is not automatic.
- **LIKELY:** normalization is both a runtime tool and a mechanistic probe of query-dependent score geometry.
- **UNKNOWN:** whether training a query-swap objective internalizes the computation or merely imitates its outputs.

### Observed-state intervention routing

Reference: `core-exp@51124de`, especially the v7/v8/v9 receipts and the v10/v11 runners inspected at that immutable commit.

- **PROVEN:** the frozen v7 observed-only three-action policy (`NO_CHANGE`, `CONSTRAINED`, `NORMALIZED`) scored 310/480 against 274/480 for the best tested fixed policy on a fresh mixed-causal fixture: +7.5 percentage points, reported 95% CI [+5.2,+10.0], `p<1e-6`.
- **REPLICATED:** the same frozen policy scored 291/480 against 248/480 on a second fresh fixture: +9.0 points, reported 95% CI [+6.5,+11.5].
- **TRANSFERRED:** on the nearby SFT7 checkpoint it scored 320/480 against 280/480: +8.3 points, reported 95% CI [+6.0,+10.8].
- **PROMISING:** observable score geometry contains reusable information about which of those three interventions will repair an output.
- **CONTAMINATED:** v10/v11 consult `len(candidates)` before assigning the current task's `candidates = task["candidates"]`. Pair-action availability therefore depends on stale previous-loop state. Their `EXACT_PAIR`/`SLOT_PAIR`, pair-composition, and claimed later promotion results are not hard evidence.
- **CONTAMINATED:** those expanded runs omit complete fixed pair-action baselines, and their per-family `best_const` is a per-case old-action oracle rather than one fixed family policy. Exact trainers for some frozen policy artifacts are also absent from committed source.
- **INVALID:** the historical bank of 166 claimed VIEs does not meet the stated one-variable intervention contract; outcome-conditioned deletion in the development harvest further invalidates use as a qualified causal bank.
- **PROVEN, narrowly:** lambda-zero policies demonstrate repair-success routing. They do not establish minimum-cost intervention selection, self-modeling, or native Core composition.
- **UNKNOWN:** how much of v7's policy is general cognitive-state diagnosis versus fixture-family structure. V5 must preserve structure-only baselines and cross-domain transfer.

### Combined internal conclusion

The strongest V5 clue is not a particular V4 architecture component. It is the causal pattern:

```text
same facts + changed query
→ changed candidate score geometry
→ intervention-specific repair can be predicted from observed state
→ free realization may still fail
```

V5 should train and measure this chain directly while keeping runtime assistance distinct from native capability. **LIKELY**

## Public research connected to V5 decisions

### Scale and data

- [Chinchilla](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.15556) supports scaling parameters and data together and provides the rough 20-token-per-parameter reference. **PROVEN / STRONG EVIDENCE** for average-loss compute allocation; **UNKNOWN** for cognition-optimal ratios.
- [DataComp-LM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.11794) shows that filtering and mixture quality materially affect model quality, and that data-method rankings at 400M/1B correlated strongly with 7B results. **PROVEN / STRONG EVIDENCE** for using proxy models to screen data.
- [DoReMi](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.10429) shows small proxies can optimize domain mixtures. **LIKELY** useful for V5 after domains and evaluation are honest.
- [Phi-1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.11644) demonstrates the efficiency potential of high-quality synthetic data, while [model-collapse evidence](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07566-y) warns against recursive model-generated distributions. This supports executable synthetic generators, not synthetic volume by itself.

### Dense Transformers versus alternatives

- [How Transformers Learn Variable Binding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.20896) causally identifies a learned residual-stream/addressable-memory mechanism and specialized attention routing for dereference chains. **PROVEN / STRONG EVIDENCE** that explicit memory is not required for controlled binding.
- [Mamba](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.00752), [Griffin](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.19427), and [Titans](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.00663) provide serious recurrent/state/memory alternatives with long-context efficiency. **SUGGESTIVE** for future ESOES scale; none directly establishes better query-conditioned binding per compute at ~200M under An-Ra's 4k target.
- [Depth and compositional generalization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.19956) supports a directional depth advantage with diminishing returns. **LIKELY** justification for a deep/narrow candidate, not for an exact layer count.
- [GQA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.13245), [RoPE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.09864), [RMSNorm](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.07467), and [SwiGLU](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.05202) support a low-risk dense baseline.

### Composition and cognition data

- [Compositional Capabilities of Autoregressive Transformers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.12997) shows autoregressive Transformers can acquire compositional operations, that intermediate outputs can improve unseen composition, and that training-order bias can destroy systematicity. **PROVEN / STRONG EVIDENCE** in controlled synthetic tasks; natural transfer remains **UNKNOWN**.
- [Grokked Transformers are Implicit Reasoners](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.15071) reports delayed in-distribution generalization without reliable OOD systematicity. This forbids using train or same-entity generalization as proof of composition.
- [Reasoning or Reciting?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.02477) shows broad degradation under counterfactual task variants. Counterfactual rules and queries belong in evaluation and training controls.
- [Skill-It](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.14430) supports prerequisite-aware ordering in some settings, while [curriculum LM results](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.02170) find no general benefit. Curriculum remains experimental.

### Representation and tokenization

- [Tokenization Is More Than Compression](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.18376) finds compression alone does not predict downstream quality.
- [Tokenization Counts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.14903) shows numerical tokenization creates consequential arithmetic inductive biases.
- [Tokenization Consistency Matters](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.09912) shows context/answer segmentation inconsistencies can harm extractive behavior.
- [Byte Latent Transformer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.09871) makes byte-level modeling a credible future alternative, but changes too many variables for the first V5 baseline.

Therefore vocabulary size, byte fallback, number segmentation, context/answer consistency, code identifiers, and nonce copying must be evaluated together. **PROVEN / STRONG EVIDENCE** for the test requirement; exact tokenizer remains **UNKNOWN**.

### Context and evaluation

- [Lost in the Middle](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.03172) and [RULER](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.06654) show nominal context length does not guarantee usable retrieval and composition.
- [GSM-Symbolic](https://openreview.net/forum?id=AjXkRZIvjB) shows small perturbations can expose fragile reasoning.

V5 must evaluate position, length, distractors, surface form, symbols, topology, and difficulty separately and jointly. **PROVEN / STRONG EVIDENCE**

## Evidence constraints carried into Ground Blueprint v0.4

1. Loss is necessary but never sufficient.
2. Final checkpoint is never automatically promoted.
3. Real updates, optimizer continuity, exact tokens, and parameter identity must be proven.
4. Representation, selection, realization, and runtime repair are separate measurements.
5. Counterfactual pairs must isolate one causal variable.
6. Sealed fixtures cannot influence training or selection and remain sealed.
7. Synthetic gains require fresh-generator and natural-domain transfer.
8. Composition must beat matched direct-retrieval controls.
9. A Connector result is not native Core capability.
10. Major scale follows replicated proxy and mid-scale evidence, not ambition.
11. No pair/composition objective may be justified from EXP v10/v11 until the stale-candidate bug, baselines, and reproduction gaps are corrected prospectively.
12. The 250M center is a user-directed implementation envelope with an exact receipt, not evidence that 195M was capacity-limited; E2/E5 retain authority over scale efficiency.
13. A short real-update canary must test optimizer ownership, non-no-op parameter/moment changes, exact step advancement, and save/resume equivalence. BF16 compute therefore uses FP32 master parameters until a deliberate low-precision-state experiment earns promotion; native BF16 AdamW moments are a known negative control.
