# Research evidence carried into Signac

This is a compact index into the existing immutable evidence, not a replacement for its preregistrations, source bundles, or postmortems. Results retain their original scope and claim ceiling.

| Lineage | Finding | Design consequence | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cymek / CYR-GPU-011** | Compact 19-symbol representation reached 56.47% held-out at 44.89% exposure; production 24,576-token BPE reached 0% held-out and 0/48 sealed at full exposure. | Treat representation and formation as first-order variables; do not assume more exposure or lower loss yields cognition. | Developmental arithmetic surface; not natural-language transfer. |
| **Cymek / CYR-GPU-012 R1** | At 512k semantic presentations, V19=12.94%, V4096=100%, V24576=0% in the reported seed. | Class-space size can change formation; retain exact 24,576 as the cross-scale comparison, while treating alternatives only as prospective experiments. | One main seed; no production tokenizer authorization. |
| **Cymek / CYR-GPU-013 R1B** | Fresh two-seed response curve was non-monotonic and seed-sensitive; V4096 was the most stable intermediate at the early endpoint. | Include multiple seeds and preserve representation identity in every comparison. | Does not establish a universal optimum or natural-language transfer. |
| **Cymek / CYR-GPU-014 R1C** | The readiness snapshot describes the mechanism experiment as complete negative for the sufficiency of softmax competition; the current readiness gate still requires metric audit and a baseline-capability gate after S5. | Do not promote masking or claim vocabulary optimality. Follow the current branch's explicit next-step gate. | This repository snapshot contains a current-state summary, not a general representation law. |
| **FORMATION-MUX-001 S5** | 24/24 arms completed on Kaggle T4×2 in 8.14 h, but identity AUC was 0–0.008 across arms; all CS-MECH runs clipped every update. Formal outcomes remain NULL; postmortem corrects interpretation to `INCONCLUSIVE_AT_ZERO_BASELINE`. | Require cheap multi-seed baseline capability/sensitivity gate before a factorial mechanism campaign. Treat clip fraction as a diagnostic. | Not an engineering failure; no causal mechanism conclusion. See `docs/cymek/experiments/FORMATION-MUX-001/observed/S5_KAGGLE_2026-09-15/POSTMORTEM.md`. |
| **BRAMASTRA** | EOS supervision moved complete-answer outcomes from 0/32 to 32/32 across two seeds. High aggregate accuracy also coexisted with 62/64 query swaps receiving the same answer. | Supervise answer plus EOS; evaluate query control directly instead of trusting aggregate accuracy. | Controlled proxy task; not broad reasoning evidence. |
| **Citadel / T1D** | Held-out arithmetic exact remained 0–6.6% across low-budget arms; teacher primitive accuracy reached 51.5% while composed behavior stayed near zero. Missing EOS in an earlier path confounded generation. | Separate primitive acquisition, composition, and realization; retain EOS in the training contract. | Low-budget runs do not establish an architecture ceiling. |
| **Arkenstone / ARK-007R, 010, 011, 015, 017** | Lower LR or sparse support can protect already acquired micro-skills; higher plasticity can help recovery; narrow support can erase invariance despite canonical accuracy. | Measure acquisition and retention separately; include support/replay and plasticity diagnostics in a future prospective design. | Effects are regime-specific; no universal LR switch or production controller is established. |
| **Arkenstone / ARK-018, 019** | Specialized rehearsal missed its internalization threshold and slowed acquisition; the V3.1 Guardian did not form a new skill. A V4 result is transcribed but raw bundle is absent. | Keep continual-learning control external and blocked pending auditable transfer evidence. | Do not treat transcribed-only results as verified raw evidence. |
| **ESOES** | Continuation lowered language loss without lifting tested cognition probes above chance. | Loss is an optimization diagnostic, never the capability promotion metric. | Probe- and regime-bounded negative. |
| **Triquetra** | An earlier readiness gate produced a false green; corrected gate distinguishes insufficient and non-identifiable evidence. | Readiness must fail closed and name what is actually measured. | Applies to that gate's instrument and tested checkpoints. |
