## Generic prior vs An-Ra evidence

- **Generic prior:** ~20 tokens/param compute-optimal planning; scaling improves loss. BOTH are budgeting inputs only — the program's own founding negative (loss ≠ cognition) forbids treating them as capability predictions.
- **An-Ra-specific modifiers:**
  1. The formation bottleneck is representation/output-space-linked (0%↔100% at fixed geometry) — scaling through an unresolved representation regime risks paying 500M-token prices for a 0%-held-out outcome (CYR-011 production arm is exactly that outcome at micro scale).
  2. Data gate: no production corpus exists; the only cognition corpus is 0.004× of 500M demand (CITADEL-DATA-001). Until CORPUS-REGEN + materialization, large-token budgets are unavailable anyway.
  3. Evaluation gate: no cognition promotion while Citadel readiness is NOT_READY.
  4. Decisive-experiment economics: every open question (R1C, transfer, Guardian) is answerable at ≤ 38M-class models on free-T4-class hardware; scale buys no information on them.
  5. Retention-law scale-transfer is unknown (U05) — a scale jump without the two-dose/fork instrumentation would produce uninterpretable retention data.
