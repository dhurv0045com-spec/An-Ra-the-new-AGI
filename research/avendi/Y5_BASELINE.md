Each panel uses distinct deterministic graph instances, independently traversed by the Y5 walker, with shuffled valid row order and distractors. This is typed input with supplied depth and a categorical value-copy transition, not byte-language understanding. The 32-case 5/6-hop scout has no observed headroom; a larger independent panel is needed to bound rare failures, and the matched training comparison is gated rather than claiming an intervention benefit.

The [frozen donor replay](../../tools/avendi_y4_frozen_donor.py) restores actual soft first-hop donor states. The original donor generator's first hop reaches the wrong state; the replay renames that node through the donor graph and validates the entire donor and recipient paths. For each true-step and original IIT seed-7 model, all 256 pairs at each depth 2, 3 and 4 passed actual-donor and independently injected oracle-state endpoints, both original and rewired suffixes, and wrong-donor actual suffix; wrong-donor intended-target counts were 0/256 per depth. Identity agreement was 256/256. The receipt retains each count; this is a new exploratory replay and not proof that the original IIT training fixtures were valid.
