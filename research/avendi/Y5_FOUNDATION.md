# Avendi V8-Y5 | Foundation, repair and consolidation

## Decision for the owner

Preserve the Y4 categorical executor. Put the next GPU comparison into the reader's handling of equivalent graph syntax. In the archived five-seed replay, changing the renderer moved the connected reader and true-step executor from 632/640 complete families to 20/640. Supplying the correct parse let the executor solve the same structure. On a fresh, bounded seed-7 intake the familiar parser scored 32/32 and the bracket parser 2/32. This isolates a consequential point of failure before the executor. It does not establish whether punctuation, position or exposure is sufficient to explain it. [Y4 checkpoint replay](../../Experiment%20Result/Avendi-V8-Y4/y4-owner-20261125/checkpoint-replay/REPORT.md) · [Y5 baseline and reproduction](Y5_BASELINE.md)

The other high-value investment is checking whether the *task* and *model readout* permit the claimed behavior. A program that answers all 280 Y4 information worlds without buying information is a counterexample to the benchmark. A correct end answer after an invalid donor intervention is equally inconclusive. Even clean, independently scored data can demand a behavior the registered head cannot express. Each of these failures can be detected before the first optimization step. ## Assessment of the evidence

| Observation | What it establishes | What would change the conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Frozen ordinary and true-step categorical models solve the typed, supplied-depth panels. The true-step model passes actual-donor suffix interventions. | There is a reusable structured operator. Intervening on the state can change the answer as computed by an independent walk. | A verified checkpoint fails on correctly represented, independently solved graphs; compare failures by operator and depth before changing the backbone. |
| Frozen reader handles native syntax and fails under a small punctuation change. | The public-to-structured interface explains much of the connected failure. | Training on matched varied syntax substantially improves **unseen** exact parsing while native performance and oracle-executor accuracy hold. Improvement only on the taught renderers supports narrower format coverage. |
| Old byte models solved zero complete families; historical training omitted an assignment and the sham duplicated useful information. | The old result measures performance of that implementation. It cannot decide whether pointer supervision helps. | A repeat with both edge assignments, a count-matched randomized sham, full-answer and EOS scoring isolates the auxiliary target. |
| Old memory feedback performed slightly worse than no feedback; the benchmark, writer and reader changed together. | There is no demonstrated useful self-correction. | Under identical public input, useful writes improve later answers; deleting the write removes the benefit and replacing it causes the predicted answer change. |
| Some jobs completed without reconstructible weights, generated responses or sealed selection. | An execution counter supports scheduling. It cannot qualify a mechanism. | Restore the registered object, regenerate its predictions, independently reconstruct the endpoint and retain the complete matched cohort. |

The smallest useful scientific unit here is a matched contrast with an independent target, a positive control and an observation that would falsify it. A thousand row permutations from one graph still represent one graph. Analyze a complete family first and preserve variation across seed pairs. A three-seed interval is imprecise. A change in multiple correlated metrics on the same cases provides little independent replication.

## Campaign and decision order

The pinned job inventory has **66 development records**. Of these, 54 are potentially trainable, six are frozen C baseline/scout evaluations, and six are Y1 recovery attempts. Confirmation restores only completed trained objects. Seeds 7, 19 and 41 are paired. Each arm reuses its matching data order and initialization or the same frozen parent where applicable. The raw records expose exposure, denominators and condition identity. Run time depends on the actual gates and available hardware.

| Priority and question | Design and evidence to inspect | Decision after confirmation |
|---|---|---|
| A. Can the existing reader learn stable relations across formats? | Same Y4 span reader, graph families, seed, 1,024 updates and batch size 16. Train fixed versus three renderers. Evaluate both on four renderers, including one withheld throughout training. Retain predicted span IDs, the decoded graph, oracle walk, frozen executor and per-family exact parse. | Inspect familiar retention and matched unseen gains for all seeds. If taught formats improve but the fourth collapses, vary one punctuation or ordering factor at a time with the frozen weights. Do not expand the executor. |
| B. Does corrected ordinary learning bind the query and both assignments? | CE, true role labels and count-matched shuffled role labels, fixed and varied training, 18 arms. Identical head shapes; CE contributes zero auxiliary gradient. Exact multi-byte answer plus EOS; full 8-cell family; same evaluation families at initialization, quarter, half and end. | Report error by depth, assignment and row reversal. A first-byte hit followed by wrong byte or absent EOS fails. If pointer and sham improve equally, evidence favors a nonspecific training effect. If case learning precedes complete-family learning, locate the earliest counterfactual failure. |
| C. Is additional corrected donor supervision useful? | Verify ordinary checkpoint and separately replay true-step. At depths 3–4 require at least .90 per depth; scout depths 5–6. Only if ordinary has at least .10 combined error continue with endpoint-only, corrected donor, alternate valid donor. Same ordinary parent, training graphs and forward shapes. Independently solve donor intermediate, recipient suffix, rewire, identity and wrong-donor targets. | A ceiling is a result about headroom. It supplies no estimate of incremental training benefit. At non-ceiling inspect both endpoints and actual-state interventions. The alternate valid donor changes supervision and is an active control. |
| D. Does the policy buy useful missing information at the right price and use it? | Opposite truths share exactly the public tuple. Every public-only rule scores at most half on the balanced pair. A .75-reliable tool yields expected utility .70 at cost .05 and .40 at .35; direct gives .50. A .50-reliable tool never pays at either price. Match costs, menu orders and terminal training. | Compare actual paid utility to the per-environment optimum and inspect low/high cost action and post-query prediction. A higher reward in the more informative environment alone does not identify a better policy. |
| E. Can a write cause an appropriate future behavioral change? | Two public binary laws. Existing GRU writes, context is emptied, a separate read answers. Relevant vs balanced sham training; matched no/irrelevant/wrong feedback, erasure and edit at evaluation. Held-out combinations use familiar public primitives. | Read relevant, edited, erased, irrelevant and no-feedback outcomes in the same worlds. A constant policy can look stable after erasure. It fails the edit and improvement gates. An engineered rule application limits the claim to storing and using the diagnostic evidence. |
| F. Can one genuinely acquired capability coexist with a qualified old one? | Use a measured qualified answer parent, or independently qualify a frozen Y4 reader on development. Fork unrestricted new-task training, the same training with a .001 relative norm cap, and equal-work old-task continuation. Reversed-answer instruction is distinct and visible. Record old and new complete-family endpoints and parameter displacement. | Require the new behavior to improve from its measured starting level and both final endpoints to qualify. A tiny parameter change plus a flat new curve is suppressed acquisition. Stratify results by parent type. The experiment concerns this simple new operation. |
| G. Can the six historical objects be recovered and attributed? | Look for exact expected Y1 manifests and weights; require source-aligned raw reference cases and complete historical parity. Y5 binds actual code, plan, evaluations and checkpoint hashes. | Record missing objects as blocked. Later recovery can revise the registry with lineage and parity evidence. A reconstructed model or a summary archive cannot replace the original. |

A/B and the C baseline establish representation and elementary operation. D and E can answer their own bounded questions in parallel. F needs a qualified parent. A joined reading, decision, memory and preservation demonstration would require independently qualified components and a fresh, end-to-end challenge. Y5 does not allocate GPUs to that claim.

## Protocol constraints and follow-through

- AB changes punctuation and text length together. Match updates, graph exposures, batch size and model, record framed token counts, and inspect format-specific errors. A positive transfer contrast alone cannot identify punctuation as the unique cause.
- The D readout must represent semantic actions independently of their displayed order. E must allow the same remembered bit to have opposite effects under the two public laws. An additive linear score over independent query and memory features cannot implement binary XOR. A known-weight witness and a public-only impossibility bound are complementary checks: the task excludes the shortcut and admits the desired solution.
- Distinguish complete-family accuracy, accurately recovered structure and connected answer accuracy. A lucky endpoint can arise from a bad parse. For C, report conditional on measured scout eligibility. For D/E, report results on the compact public encodings and within the finite law/channel set.
- Source, model, cohort and per-case evidence travel together. Inspect the evidence bundle first. Use FULL for resume, weight-level replay, alternate interventions or a fresh diagnosis of the same weights. Protect confirmation as a one-time choice of cohort within the run; generating a new plan after seeing its cases requires a fresh confirmation set.
- If a foundation fails, first compare construction, exposure, representation, optimization and execution on an identical fixture. Extend the permanent register with the evidence and a discriminating test. A new architecture deserves a trial only after a concrete input/output mapping or capacity limitation of the existing mechanism can be shown.
