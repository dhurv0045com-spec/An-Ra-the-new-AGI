The planned fresh-weight V7 runs are new experiments, not re-evaluations of frozen V6 checkpoints.

Fourteen recorded S3 one-epoch training runs completed. The 15 primary panels reported two PASS, ten FAIL and three NOT_QUALIFIED; four supplements were exploratory. The best individual arm, A3 seed 19, reached 62/112 (55.4%), below the registered 90% aggregate and 80%-per-kind formation gate. These results establish that the screen ran and contain useful narrow signals, not that broad capabilities were acquired. There were no completed S8/S20, independent replication-stage, or sequential A/B/C training records in the inspected arm report.

The principal diagnostic is output collapse: A1 seed 7 answered `I` in all 73 episodes terminating with a value answer, with 1/74 non-null targets correct and 38/38 null targets correctly abstained. It made the useful first query on all 32 missing/conflict cases but answered 0/26 non-null targets in those cases correctly; its six correct terminal outcomes were null abstentions. P06 reported 0/32 both-correct payload pairs. The offline audit joined labels from an explicitly privileged oracle after execution; those labels were not actor inputs. These observations motivate separating query selection, evidence delivery and value extraction, but do not identify the cause of collapse.

A3 seed 19 obtained 14/16 memory and 7/16 route outcomes, versus 5/16 and 0/16 for seed 7; neither queried. This is an unstable lead, not a demonstrated general retrieval capability. The reported P14 causal-memory failure belongs to A1 seed 7 and cannot be assigned to A3. The V6 A3 uncertainty-first ordering grouped examples at the start of epoch 0, while no intermediate evaluations established that querying was acquired and then lost. V7 measures the trajectory before asserting forgetting. The V6 supplemental thinking and stale-memory results likewise do not establish a reason to scale or a robust memory mechanism.

The 14 training workers recorded **2,348.711 GPU reservation seconds** (39.15 GPU-minutes), excluding qualification, diagnostics, export and other overhead. The ordinary one-epoch corpus contained 9,472 action examples and 18,944 supervised target tokens; 2.2 MB of prompt/target text is not 2.2 MB of supervised targets. These costs do not describe the full end-to-end campaign.

V6's strict assertions and raw receipts remain unchanged. This engineering repair does not explain wrong model answers or establish original weight custody.
