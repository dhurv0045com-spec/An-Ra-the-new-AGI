## Findings and limits

**The counterfactual margin helped R on all three seeds.** Against the identical architecture already receiving the consistency loss (D2 versus D1), R gained 1.43, 4.04 and 2.13 percentage points in overall accuracy. Among the 2,304 final views per seed, 113/80, 225/132 and 190/141 changed from wrong to correct / correct to wrong. The mean gain was 2.53 points, with a 3.96-point gain on edit pairs. Those paired counts show substantial regressions alongside the net gain. P under the same loss change moved −1.74, +2.52 and −3.95 points, averaging −1.06. The difference in response between R and P was positive on each seed (+3.17, +1.52, +6.08). This supports an architecture-dependent training effect on these runs. Whether R's **cross-slot messages** caused it remains unresolved. The paired-objective runs lack an R-noedge comparator.

**The strongest paired objective still left a large deficit against A0.** D2 R exceeded D0 R by 6.96 points overall and 13.65 points on edit pairs. It reached 64.32% on five-hop stress, above A0 R's 58.33%. Yet its overall score was 47.64% against A0 R's 61.65%, and its narrative score was only 9.81% against 25.43%. D2 P likewise reached 39.99% overall against A0 P's 62.02%. Equal update counts do not equalize the number of distinct underlying worlds per batch. The paired generator draws linked presentations together and retains only decision stages common to all four trajectories. World diversity and post-tool training coverage are plausible explanations that this archive cannot separate.

**Where R improved and where it regressed.** From D0 to D2, the largest mean family gain was X07 (retrieving a missing edge), +18.2 points. X10 fell 5.6 points and X12 fell 15.6. Across the 6,912 final views per condition, the recorded first-divergence category “wrong value with the same operation” fell from 2,098 to 1,950, and wrong initial actions fell from 221 to 77. Post-tool answer failures rose from 640 to 658. The three-view conjunction (correct on canonical, code permutation and irrelevant-fact variants of the same world) improved from 32.20% to 43.58%. Narrative rendering remained weak. These exploratory breakdowns locate failure cases for a controlled test. They give no independent confirmation of traversal or belief revision.

**The repaired B0 establishes the missing baseline.** J averaged 3.17 points above B0. The differences were +0.91, +9.07 and −0.48, so a broad advantage for J is uncertain. On the common D1-H protocol, prior A0 P scored 62.02%, A0 R 61.65%, and the active F-cap control 28.10%. A typed action interface alone did not reproduce P's overall performance. B0 and J nevertheless beat A0 P on narrative rendering (20.31% and 26.22% versus 16.15%). Model choice depends on the tested presentation. Three training seeds and linked world views limit inference beyond these cohorts.
